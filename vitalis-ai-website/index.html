01 / THE MISSION

Science moves forward
when knowledge connects.

Biomedical knowledge is spread across papers, datasets, experiments, and disciplines. VITALIS AI aims to explore tools that help make this information easier to navigate and reason about.

The long-term vision is to build a language model and research workflow that can work with scientific text, preserve source context, and help people investigate questions more systematically.

A / 01
⌘

Learn from evidence

Build around traceable sources, useful datasets, and careful evaluation—not confident-sounding guesses.

A / 03

04 / RESPONSIBLE BY DESIGN

Ambition needs
accountability.

Biomedical AI can be useful, but it can also be wrong. VITALIS AI is a research project—not a medical device or a substitute for a qualified healthcare professional.

01
Evidence over certainty

Distinguish established findings, uncertain results, and new hypotheses.

02
Privacy matters

Do not train on identifiable patient records without proper authorization and safeguards.

03
Validate before use

Scientific and medical outputs need independent review and appropriate validation.
